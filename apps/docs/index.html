What is Honey Network?

A New Blockchain Built for DeFi

The Honey Network is a custom-built Layer 1 blockchain designed from the ground up for DeFi applications. With 4-second block times, sub-cent gas fees, and native AMM liquidity pools, it delivers the speed and economics that real DeFi demands.

Built on an Ed25519 BIP39 keypair architecture, every wallet is non-custodial and fully self-sovereign. Your 12-word seed phrase controls your assets — no intermediaries, no custodians.

Honey Network uses Pyth Network oracle feeds for live, manipulation-resistant price data — the same oracle infrastructure used by the largest protocols in DeFi.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Honey Network? ▼
The Honey Network is a custom Layer 1 blockchain built specifically for DeFi. It features 4-second block times, sub-cent gas fees, a native AMM DEX, liquid staking, and real-time price feeds from the Pyth Network oracle. The current release is a public testnet — all tokens are for testing only and have no real-world value.
Is HIVE Wallet non-custodial? Who holds my keys? ▼
Yes — HIVE Wallet is fully non-custodial. Your private keys are derived from a 12-word BIP39 seed phrase and are stored locally on your device using encrypted secure storage. Your keys never leave your device and no server ever has access to them. Your seed phrase is the only way to recover your wallets — store it somewhere safe.
What are the transaction fees on Honey Network? ▼
Gas fees start at 0.00000001 HNY (one satoshi equivalent), making them effectively free for most users. A service fee of 0.0005% of the transfer USD value is charged on sends and swaps — this is far below any major blockchain. Of each service fee collected, 75% goes to the network fee vault and 25% is credited to HIVE Wallet holders. Transfers between your own sub-wallets cost only the base gas fee.
How does staking work? What is stHNY? ▼
Stake your HNY to receive stHNY — a liquid staking token representing your principal plus continuously accruing rewards at 5% APR. Unlike traditional staking, stHNY is freely swappable and can also be deposited into the stHNY-HNY liquidity pool for additional 8% APR in HNY rewards (13% combined). To unstake, initiate an unlock and wait 7 days for the unbonding period.
What are liquidity pools and LPHNY? ▼
Liquidity pools are token pairs (e.g., HNY-ETH, HNY-BTC) that power the native AMM DEX. When you deposit tokens into a pool you receive LPHNY — an LP token representing your proportional share of the pool. LPHNY accrues 8% APR in HNY rewards in real-time, claimable at any time. You can also send LPHNY to another wallet, which automatically splits your LP position proportionally across pools.
How does the swap work? What is price impact? ▼
Swaps use a Uniswap v2-style constant product AMM (x*y=k). The swap price is determined by the current pool reserves — the larger your swap relative to the pool, the higher your price impact. A 0.10% pool fee is charged on each swap and stays in the pool, earning for liquidity providers. Multi-hop routing is supported, allowing swaps between token pairs that don't share a direct pool.
Where do the live token prices come from? ▼
Real-time prices for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, and other tokens come from the Pyth Network — the same oracle infrastructure used by Solana DeFi, Synthetix, Aave, and over 200 other protocols. Prices update every ~60 seconds and are used for USD valuations in your portfolio, service fee calculations, and LP position values. HNY pricing is set by the protocol for testnet purposes.
Can I use HIVE Wallet on my phone? ▼
HIVE Wallet is built with React Native + Expo, which means native iOS and Android apps are in development. For now, the web app at localhost:8081 works on both desktop and mobile browsers. The mobile-optimized interface is fully touch-friendly. Native app store releases are planned for Phase 4 of the roadmap.
Are testnet tokens worth anything? ▼
No — all tokens on the Honey Network testnet (HNY, stHNY, LPHNY, ETH, BTC, SOL, USDT, USDC, XRP) are testnet tokens for development and testing purposes only. They have zero real-world monetary value. The faucet lets you mint unlimited tokens to explore the wallet's features freely. Real token economics will be established at mainnet launch.
